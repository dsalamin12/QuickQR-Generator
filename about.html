About QuickQR

Last updated: July 2026

Our Mission

QuickQR was created to provide a fast, free, and privacy-first QR Code Generator without forcing users to sign up or create accounts. We believe data security should be standard across utility applications.

Privacy First

Unlike conventional tools, your QR configurations are handled safely through client-side technology. All codes generate instantly inside your browser viewport, meaning your sensitive structural content never uploads to external cloud databases.

Features

  • URL QR Codes
  • WiFi QR Codes
  • WhatsApp QR Codes
  • vCard QR Codes
  • Email QR Codes
  • SMS QR Codes
  • PNG & SVG Downloads
  • Bulk QR Generation
  • Logo & Color Customization

Our Goal

We continuously expand QuickQR by introducing advanced tool capabilities, high-intent technical documentation blueprints, and operational updates while keeping the primary generator interface free and private for worldwide users.

Who Runs QuickQR

QuickQR is independently built and maintained by the QuickQR Team. We are not a large company — QuickQR is run as a focused, single-purpose tool rather than a broad platform, which is why it stays fast, simple, and free of unnecessary accounts or tracking.

If you'd like to know more about who's behind QuickQR, or have a question for us directly, see our Contact page.

How We Write Our Guides

Articles on the QuickQR blog are written to answer real, practical questions — how to set up a WiFi QR code for a restaurant, how to put a QR code on a business card, and similar everyday use cases. Guides are based on hands-on testing of the QuickQR generator itself, reviewed for accuracy before publishing, and updated when QR code best practices or platform behavior changes (for example, how WhatsApp or Instagram handle QR-linked profiles).

We link out to official sources (like platform help pages) where appropriate, and we welcome corrections — if something in a guide is outdated or inaccurate, let us know via Contact and we'll fix it.
