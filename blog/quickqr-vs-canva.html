QuickQR vs Canva — Which QR Code Generator Should You Use?

QuickQR Guide  ·  Free  ·  4 min read

Canva's QR code tool is convenient if you're already designing something inside Canva — but it comes with real restrictions worth knowing before you commit a design to print. Here's an honest, factual look at how it compares to a dedicated QR code generator like QuickQR.

Where QuickQR Wins

  • No account, no login — open the page, generate, download. Canva requires signing in before you can create or export a code
  • Free SVG export — Canva's free tier only offers PNG; SVG (needed for crisp large-format printing) sits behind a Canva Pro subscription
  • Fully client-side — your link and any logo you upload are processed entirely in your browser and never sent to a server, since QuickQR has no backend at all
  • Purpose-built — dedicated URL, WiFi, Email, SMS, WhatsApp, vCard, and text QR tools, rather than one generic app tucked inside a design editor

Where Canva Wins

  • Already in your design — if you're building a flyer or poster in Canva anyway, adding a QR code without leaving the editor saves a step
  • One-click placement into a layout — the code drops directly onto your canvas, pre-sized to fit alongside other design elements

Which Should You Use?

If you're already deep into a Canva design and just need a simple static URL code to drop in, Canva's built-in tool is fine for that narrow case. For anything printed at large size, anything needing a logo, or if you'd rather not create an account or send your link through a third party's servers, QuickQR is the more capable — and more private — choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Canva's QR code generator require an account?

Yes. You need to be signed into Canva to generate and download a QR code, even for a single one-off code.

Can I get an SVG file from Canva for free?

No — SVG export is limited to Canva's paid Pro plan. The free tier exports PNG only.

Does QuickQR store or track the links I generate?

No. QuickQR runs entirely in your browser — your link, colors, and any logo you upload are never sent to a server or stored anywhere.
