SMS QR Codes: Let Customers Text You Instantly

QuickQR Guide  ·  Free  ·  4 min read

Not everyone uses WhatsApp — but almost every phone can send a text message. An SMS QR code opens the phone's native messaging app with your number (and optionally a message) already filled in. One scan, no app to install, works on any phone.

Who Should Use an SMS QR Code?

  • Real estate agents — "Text for info" on yard signs and listing flyers
  • Retailers & service businesses — especially in regions where SMS is more common than WhatsApp
  • Event & lead-capture booths — collect enquiries without needing an app
  • Salons, clinics & appointment-based businesses — let people text to book
  • Landlords & property managers — quick tenant contact on notices

How to Create an SMS QR Code — Step by Step

Step 1: Open the SMS QR Generator

Go to QuickQR's SMS QR Code Generator. Free, no sign-up, runs entirely in your browser.

Step 2: Enter Your Phone Number

Type your number in the Phone Number field, including the country code (for example +12345678900).

Step 3: Add a Pre-Filled Message (Optional)

Use the Pre-filled Message field to start the conversation for the person scanning — for example "Hi, I'm interested in the property on Oak Street" or "I'd like to book an appointment." This removes friction and makes people more likely to actually send it.

Step 4: Customise & Download

Adjust the colour and shape to match your branding, then download as PNG for screens or SVG for print — SVG is the better choice for yard signs and large-format printing since it stays crisp at any size.

Tips for a Better SMS QR Code

  • Always include the country code — a QR code without it may fail to open the messaging app correctly on a phone abroad
  • Keep the pre-filled message short and specific — one clear sentence works better than a long paragraph
  • Test on both iPhone and Android before printing — messaging apps can behave slightly differently between platforms
  • Add a short instruction near the code, like "Scan to text us," so people know what to expect

Frequently Asked Questions

Does texting me cost the person anything?

Standard SMS or carrier messaging rates may apply on their end, the same as sending any normal text message. QuickQR has no control over carrier charges.

Do I need an SMS marketing platform for this to work?

No. The QR code simply opens the person's own default messaging app with your number filled in — it's a direct, one-to-one text, not a marketing platform or automated system.

Is my phone number stored anywhere?

No. QuickQR runs entirely in your browser. Your number is encoded directly into the QR code and never sent to or stored on any server.
