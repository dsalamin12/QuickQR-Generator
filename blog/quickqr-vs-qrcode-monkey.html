QuickQR vs QRCode Monkey — Which QR Code Generator Should You Use?

QuickQR Guide  ·  Free  ·  4 min read

QRCode Monkey is a genuinely capable free QR code generator, and it deserves credit for that — no account required, strong design options, and full vector export at no cost. The real difference between it and QuickQR comes down to what happens to your data along the way, and how the two tools are built.

Quick Comparison

Where QuickQR Wins

  • Fully client-side generation — QuickQR has no backend at all, so your link and logo are never transmitted anywhere, let alone stored. QRCode Monkey generates the code on its own servers
  • Purpose-built tool pages — dedicated WiFi, vCard, Email, SMS, and WhatsApp generators rather than one generic multi-type form
  • No ads, no upsell path — QRCode Monkey nudges toward its paid sibling product (QR Code Generator Pro) if you look for scan tracking; QuickQR simply doesn't offer that feature rather than gating it behind a paywall

Where QRCode Monkey Wins

  • Deeper visual customization — gradient fills, a wider range of custom eye and module shapes on the free tier are a genuine strength QuickQR doesn't try to match
  • More export formats — alongside PNG and SVG, QRCode Monkey also offers PDF and EPS output (though these two drop the logo and design styling)
  • Broadest range of content types — beyond the common formats, it supports extras like Bitcoin addresses and social profile links

Which Should You Use?

If your priority is maximum visual design control — gradients, unusual eye shapes, exotic content types — QRCode Monkey's free tier genuinely delivers that. If your priority is knowing your link and logo never leave your device, QuickQR's fully client-side, no-backend approach is the more private option, with no meaningful gap in the basics: free logo upload, free SVG export, no account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is QRCode Monkey really free?

Yes, for static codes — unlimited generation, no account, and free SVG/PNG export with your logo and design intact. Its "statistics and editability" option redirects to a separate paid product, but the core static generator stays free.

Does QRCode Monkey store the QR codes I create?

QRCode Monkey generates codes on its own servers rather than in your browser, which means your link and any uploaded logo pass through a third party to produce the code.

Does QuickQR support gradient colors like QRCode Monkey?

Not currently — QuickQR focuses on solid-color customization and logo upload rather than gradient fills or custom eye shapes. If deep visual design is your priority, QRCode Monkey has the edge there.
